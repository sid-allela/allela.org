Allela

The Operational Flight Recorder for AI.

What we do

Allela is an independent evidence layer for AI decisions. Each decision you instrument is hashed inside your own environment, signed, and timestamped independently by DigiCert. If anyone changes the record later, the change can be detected.

Only hashes and non-sensitive metadata leave your environment. Raw prompts and outputs never do.

What we don't do

Allela proves your records haven't been altered. It doesn't explain why a model made a decision, and it doesn't, on its own, make you compliant with any regulation.

Who it's for

Compliance and risk teams at EU companies running high-risk AI: credit decisioning, insurance underwriting, and emergency triage.
